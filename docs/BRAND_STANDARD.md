# Veyra master-brand standard

**Brand name:** Veyra  
**Display wordmark:** VEYRA  
**Approved tagline:** Imagine it. Build it. Own it.  
**Brand philosophy:** Vision Evolved. Your Reach Amplified.  
**Ownership credit:** Powered by Timzee Corp

These are intentionally created brand interpretations and phrases, not claims about dictionary etymology or verified trademark exclusivity.

## Positioning

**Veyra** is the master brand for an international creative software platform.  
**Veyra Studio** is the visual website-building product under that brand.

The experience should feel capable, thoughtful and approachable: polished enough for designers and businesses, understandable to first-time creators, and credible when the product is acquired or maintained by another engineering team.

## Brand voice

- Confident, useful and concise. Avoid exaggeration, especially around AI and "unlimited" claims.
- Write about the outcome a creator can achieve. Avoid placeholder content that masquerades as customer success.
- Name working features specifically. Distinguish **available**, **beta**, and **planned**.
- Do not imply that Veyra grants legal ownership of third-party templates, images or trademarks.
- Reinforce the principle behind **"Own it"** through editable content, access controls and progressively better export paths—not claims that full migration tooling already exists.
- Use **Powered by Timzee Corp** in the site footer, product story, and places that establish parent-company ownership. Avoid intrusive repeated marks in every toolbar.

## Interface and editorial rules

- The main headline should normally be one idea; marketing home may use the tagline verbatim.
- Use accessible semantic headings and clear button labels. Never rely on color alone for meaning.
- Treat privacy and draft safety as product features rather than hidden technical details.
- Label conceptual interface art as **illustration**, never as an actual screenshot.
- Do not use fake testimonials, invented partner logos, fabricated business statistics or artificial creator profiles.
- Keep marketing copy and onboarding copy consistent with the brand constants in `src/platform/brand/identity.ts`.
- Keep metadata, canonical URLs and indexing flags managed centrally via `src/platform/seo/site-config.ts`.

## Legal and commercial checks before launch

Confirm brand and domain availability in intended jurisdictions, intellectual-property ownership, visual-asset licensing and the suitability of "Own it" claims with qualified counsel. A proposed name or tagline is not a trademark registration.
